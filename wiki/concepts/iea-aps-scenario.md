# IEA Announced Pledges Scenario (APS)

**Definition**: IEA's Announced Pledges Scenario reflects global energy & climate policy as stated by governments (NDCs, net-zero commitments, etc.). Used in GREAT as baseline electricity & hydrogen demand assumptions.

**GREAT use**: APS demand projections drive model; GREAT's 9×9 scenario matrix then toggles flexibility & infrastructure assumptions **around** this demand baseline.

**Key claim**: APS ~6000 TWh EU27 electricity generation by 2050 (baseline); individual scenario dimensions adjust technology adoption (EV%, HP%, H₂ demand, etc.) relative to this target.

**Source**: IEA World Energy Outlook (see [[great-scenario-data-sources]] for specific review & extraction task)
